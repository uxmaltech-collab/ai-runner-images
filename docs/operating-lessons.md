# Image composition and preservation lessons

The shared host/executor and publisher remain in their existing owners. Images
owns reproducible tool recipes and qualification, not a second runtime.

- Compose native tools, one combined Codex/Claude/Grok layer, then optional
  Flutter/Dart. Preserve exact parents and qualify the changed surface only.
- Prefer a small owned derivative for a driver or companion repair. Do not
  reinstall an unchanged Xcode/SDK stack. Distinguish logical layers from repair
  ancestry and preserve historical ineligible artifacts.
- Test complete archives, wheels, companion executables and PATH as the actual
  nonroot job user. Read/search directory modes and Darwin symlink modes matter.
- GUI checks require the intended Aqua session. Root launch does not qualify
  Simulator or unattended tool permissions. Keep authentication outside images.
- Internet egress does not supply working guest DNS. Apply the declared DNS and
  resume only failed stages. Flutter uses a sealed seed and private per-user
  writable clones; neutral Hello World does not accept a customer application.
- Shutdown normally and verify persisted disk/config/NVRAM before sealing. Keep
  their hashes distinct from native manifest and OCI identities.
- Android needs exact archive locks, SDK package registration, declared KVM and
  real privilege reduction. Resolve shares the image without booting an emulator.
- Verify recorder console responses as well as process exit. The observed Android
  recording limit was 1800 seconds. Preserve complete media before resource cleanup.
- Verify decoded video and target-player playback separately. The accepted iOS
  original has an HEVC Chromium limit; Android WebM played directly. Any conversion
  is a separately identified derivative, not a silent replacement of originals.

## Distribution and restoration

A local sealed image is not a backup. The current catalog remains local-only.
Before claiming distribution, record approved storage, retrievable immutable
objects, size/hash for every component, retention ownership and access references.
Keep licensed/private payloads outside Git. Do not invent a registry reference
for a native manifest or publish organization secrets with a reusable artifact.

Restore into a separate owned location, verify each component, boot through the
canonical owner, exercise the intended unprivileged tools and observe cleanup.
Record artifact and source identities, command/results and whether that actual
restore succeeded. Preserve the original until the replacement is proven. A
checksum list or HTTP200 alone never establishes restoration.

retain accepted consumer runs and publication recovery. They do not imply neutral
recipe rebuilds, all-platform qualification or production/fleet acceptance.
